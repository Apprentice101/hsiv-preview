A GUIDE, NOT A VERDICT

How this will work

Choose the topics you want to explore. Candidate summaries will describe documented statements and actions, with evidence you can open and read yourself.

Candidates will appear alphabetically by last name. Every candidate in a covered ballot roster will remain visible, even when research is incomplete.

CLEAR ABOUT OUR LIMITS

What we do & don’t claim

HSIV will not score agreement, recommend candidates, or infer your beliefs. Missing information describes the limits of our research, not a candidate’s views.

This preview contains no real candidate research. Source standards and the publishing process are still being developed.
